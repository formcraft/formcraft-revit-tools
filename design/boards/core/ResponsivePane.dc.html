RuleNAM-012
WidthWhat changes
Under 320 pxNot allowed: the pane holds at 320 px; Revit's dock gives the rest to the view
320–399 pxCompact: icons in the header, search shortened, one column, footer buttons wrap
400–560 pxNormal: full labels, one column of fields, tables full width
Wider than 560 pxContent stops at 560 px and stays left-aligned, so lines never get too long; forms use two columns
How Revit sets it
The pane registers a minimum width with Revit when it docks. Revit decides the dock's size, so the maximum is held by the content: it stops growing at 560 px.
Why limits
Too narrow and labels wrap into nonsense; too wide and tables stretch so far the eye loses the row. 320 to 560 keeps every section readable.
