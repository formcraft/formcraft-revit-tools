Ceiling Design Assistant: the pane

Five steps in one section of the Formcraft pane, next to Casework, drawn with the UI-kit tokens. The living room is real: a 6.0 × 4.5 m tray ceiling computed into 31.86 m² of board (13 sheets), 129.9 m of channels, 30 hangers, 21 m of cornice and 16.2 m of cove LED, so every number agrees.

1 · Room and brief
Pick a room. The assistant reads the room outline, what is above (truss ties, slab, beams), lighting circuits, AC units, sprinklers and existing fixtures. Describe the ceiling, sketch on the ceiling plan or start from a template; build-ups and finishes come from the kit (I.CLG).

On the ceiling plan (Flow overlay) and in section

Designs are ghosted in blue on the RCP view; hovering a check highlights the hanger, grille or fixture. After placing: ceiling-height tags, the bulkhead symbol and the access-panel symbol (kit FMC_SYM_I_*) are added to the RCP.
