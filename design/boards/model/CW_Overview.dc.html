Casework Assistant: the best casework tool for Revit

From a room and a sentence to a coordinated kitchen in the model and a workshop pack your carpenters can cut from: real panels, real boards, real checks. One section of the Formcraft pane (Engineer › Casework, Model module), built on the recipe engine, the material library and the design-movement profile sets.

The flow

Seven capabilities

1

Room-aware layout from a sentence or a sketch

Reads walls, openings, water, waste, gas and sockets from the model; proposes three layouts with reasons and numbers; ghosts them on the plan; nothing placed until you approve.

2

Real joinery, not boxes

Every unit is panels: sides, bottom, top or rails, back, shelves, fronts, fillers, plinth. Each panel has its board, edge band and grain from the material library and the kit's joinery rules.

3

Workshop pack in one click

Cut list per assembly mark, sheet nesting on 1220 × 2440, edge-banding metres, hardware schedule, shop drawings (A3), CNC DXF, cost from the material library; fills I11_Joinery_Cutlist.

4

Coordination checks

Sink to waste, cooker clearances and gas, hood duct route, sockets, windows and sills, door routes and swings, accessibility; flags with the kit rule, never fixes a professional's decision.

5

Style follows the design

Fronts, handles, plinths and cornices come from the design-movement profile sets; change FMC_Design_Movement and the joinery follows the house.

6

Edit by conversation

"Make the drawers equal", "swap to Mvule fronts", "add a 600 bin pull-out": each change shows a dry run and updates the cut list and cost live.

7

Learns your workshop

Depths, carcass thickness, runner brand, back fixing, edge rules and how your carpenters build become kit rules; every project starts from your practice.

House rules it keeps

· Model tools run only on Setup models, new documents or staging (live-model lock); workshared models are never saved by a tool.

· Every write is a dry run first and one Undo; a log line per run.

· Boards, edges, hardware, sizes and prices come from the kit and the material library; a missing value is a kit change request, never invented.

· It never decides for a professional: gas, electrical and structural items are flagged with the rule, not changed.

· Unit families pass Gate A in staging before anyone releases them.
