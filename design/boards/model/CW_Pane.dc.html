On the Revit view (Flow overlay)

While the pane is open, the options are ghosted in blue on the plan view; hovering a check row highlights its element (the socket, the waste, the window). After placing, unit marks and the selected unit's dimensions show on the view.

Patterns used (all from the UI kit)

FmcNav.Section with the +Model FmcTag · FmcSegmentButton step bar · FmcDocBanner (Setup) · FmcButton.Ai with the AI tag · FmcSegmented · FmcComboBox fields · FmcCheckRow with FmcChip status · FmcTable panel list · FmcToggle for the overlay · FmcDialog for the dry-run approval · Ask box from Ask Claude.
