Ceiling Design Assistant

From a room and a sentence to a coordinated ceiling in the model, with the lights, fan, grilles and detectors in place, and a set-out and bill of quantities the installer can build from. The same pattern as the Casework Assistant: one section of the pane, dry run first, kit data only.

The flow

Seven capabilities

1

Reads the room and the void

Outline, heights, what is above (truss ties, slab, beams), lighting circuits, AC units and ducts, sprinklers, detectors and existing fixtures; ceiling build-ups from the kit.

2

Designs from a sentence or a sketch

Flat, tray, bulkhead, coffered, cove, floating raft, timber slats, PVC panels, T&G, 600 grid; three scored options ghosted on the RCP and in 3D.

3

Coordinated with the services

Lights, fans, AC grilles and ducts, detectors and sprinklers placed with the design; hanger and duct clashes, fan sweep and height, access to drivers and valves.

4

Buildable set-out

Board layout with numbered boards, joints staggered, no slivers; main channels, furring and hangers on their grids; setting-out point; screws, tape and skim.

5

Style follows the design

Cornices, ceiling roses, coffer mouldings and edge details from the design-movement profile sets (Crown, Panel Mould, CeilingRose).

6

Edit by conversation

"Bulkhead 900 on the TV wall", "add a raft over the dining table", "swap to T&G pine": dry run, quantities and cost update.

7

Ceiling pack

RCP sheet (VT_I_RCP), set-out drawing, section details, bill of quantities and cost from the material library; ceiling schedule fills as you model.
