For people outside the firm
Clients, contractors and consultants with no Revit seat. In the firm, QSs and managers work in Revit with the Build pane and workspace window.
Roles decide
Each role sees its own packages, progress and drawings. Rates stay hidden unless the role has the rates permission.
Changes go through approval
Site progress, photos and variations land in the data package; they reach Revit only after preview, QS approval and a log line.
Hosted on the CDE
Runs on the Formcraft CDE, with accounts that follow the Manual 7.6 permissions and expire. Data Protection Act 2019 applies.
