Revisions and reviews

Five clicks from seeing a problem to a tracked, shared item

1
Click Add revision
In the Review section of the pane, or right-click on the view.
2
Drag a box on the drawing
Works on a sheet or a view. The cloud goes on the open review revision: Revit allows clouds only on revisions not yet issued.
3
Write what you see
Type, title, what must change. Elements inside the box are linked.
4
Call in the disciplines
Pick every discipline it touches, one lead, one owner and a due date.
5
Answer and close
Each discipline answers, the lead checks the model, the item closes and shows on the next issue.
Planned design. Drawing and values are samples.
