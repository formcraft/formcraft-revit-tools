Assignment, not permission
Revit worksets can't be locked to named people. The plugin gives you the next best thing: a register of who should work where, a warning to anyone outside it before they edit, and the option for the coordinator to hold a workset.
Activity, not ranking
Your Manual (14.5) retired per-person volume counts because they reward speed over care. This view shows who is active and who has unsynced work, so the team can help each other, never a league table.
